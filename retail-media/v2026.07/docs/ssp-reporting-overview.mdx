SSP reporting is for supply accounts (retailers). It shows revenue earned from hosted ads and fill rate across placements. All endpoints are asynchronous — see [Async Patterns](/retail-media/docs/async-patterns).

<Note>
  See the full list of endpoints and parameters in the [→ API Reference](/retail-media/reference/generate-async-revenue-report)
</Note>

<Warning>
  Supply account access only. Requires the `retail-media:read` scope on a supply account. Demand account credentials will not return data from these endpoints.
</Warning>

---

## Available reports

| Report | Endpoint | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue** | `POST /reports/revenue` | Total revenue from all advertisers, broken out by advertiser, campaign, date, and format |
| **Fill Rate** | `POST /reports/fillrate` | What percentage of deliverable placements were filled, by page type, environment, or placement |
| **Unfilled Placements** | `POST /reports/unfilled-placements` | Why specific placements went unfilled — by reason code, page type, and date |

**Fill Rate vs Unfilled Placements** answer different questions about the same inventory. Use Fill Rate to track aggregate coverage trends; use Unfilled Placements to diagnose specific supply/demand gaps by reason.

---

## How revenue numbers work

The Revenue report shows what brands paid as revenue to the retailer — not what brands see in their DSP reports.

- `revenue` — gross revenue across all formats
- `netRevenue` — revenue after account fees are applied
- `workingMedia` — the brand's total ad spend (before Criteo fees); what you see as `spend` on the DSP side

These three fields are not interchangeable. If you're reconciling numbers with an advertiser, `workingMedia` and the DSP's `spend` are the closest match — but confirm with Criteo support, as they are not guaranteed to be identical.

## Constraints

- **Supply account only.** Demand accounts cannot call these endpoints.
- **`metrics` and `dimensions` are required** on every call.
- **Date range:** check per-endpoint limits in the API reference.
- Revenue data for Offsite campaigns may be delayed up to 72 hours.
