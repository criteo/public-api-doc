---
title: Monitor Campaigns & Pull Performance Data
description: Overview of the Retail Media reporting APIs — DSP for advertisers, SSP for retailers.
---

Retail Media reporting splits into two sides depending on who is calling and what they need to measure.

| Side | Who uses it | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **DSP (Demand Side)** | Brands and agencies | Campaign performance, attributed sales, missed opportunities |
| **SSP (Supply Side)** | Retailers | Revenue earned from hosted ads, fill rate, unfilled placements |

All reporting endpoints are asynchronous — you submit a request, poll for status, then download the result. See [Async Patterns](/retail-media/docs/async-patterns).
