---
title: Initialize Before Append
description: Targeting resources that require a PUT to initialize the scope before POST append or DELETE operations will work.
---

Several targeting sub-resources must be initialized with a `PUT` before you can append or remove items. Sending a `POST` or `DELETE` without prior initialization returns a 4xx error.

## Resources Requiring Initialization

| Resource | Initialize with | Then you can |
|---|---|---|
| Audience targeting | `PUT /line-items/{id}/targeting/audiences` | `POST` to append segments, `DELETE` to remove |
| Store targeting | `PUT /line-items/{id}/targeting/stores` | `POST` to append stores, `DELETE` to remove |
| Add-to-basket targeting | `PUT /line-items/{id}/targeting/add-to-basket` | `POST` to append, `DELETE` to remove |

## Additional Constraints

| Resource | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Add-to-basket targeting | `productIds` and `categoryIds` are mutually exclusive within a single scope — a scope can target one or the other, not both. |

## Diagnosing the Error

If a `POST` or `DELETE` on a targeting sub-resource returns a 4xx, check whether the scope was initialized with `PUT` first. A missing initialization is the most common cause.
