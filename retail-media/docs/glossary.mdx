---
title: Glossary
description: Definitions for the terms used across Commerce Max and the Retail Media API.
---

Several terms in the Retail Media API have specific meanings that differ from general industry usage or are easy to conflate. This page defines each one.

## Account & ecosystem

**Commerce Max (CMax)** — Criteo's retail media platform. Brands and agencies use it to activate Onsite and Offsite campaigns. Most API workflows operate within Commerce Max.

**Demand account** — an account held by a brand, agency, or seller that buys advertising. Two subtypes: Demand-Brand and Demand-Seller.

**Supply account** — an account held by a retailer that manages ad inventory. Has access to both supply-side and demand-side features in Commerce Max.

**Private Market** — a retailer-controlled campaign environment, distinct from Commerce Max. Not an account type — it describes how campaigns are activated. Campaigns appear in reporting with an `Activation platform` dimension of either `Commerce Max` or `Private Market`.

**Retailer** — a Retail Media entity identified by a `retailerId`. Distinct from a Supply account (`accountId`) — related but not the same object or identifier.

**Partner** — a label used only in the context of the Partner Billing Report. It refers to the retailer in a billing relationship where the retailer bills advertisers who contracted directly with them. Not an account type and not interchangeable with `retailerId`.

## Budgets & billing

**Balance** — an account-level spend pool that funds one or more campaigns. When a balance is exhausted, all linked campaigns stop spending. Managed in Commerce Max under Billing > Balances. Not the same as a Budget.

**Budget** — a spend ceiling set at the campaign or line item level. Caps how much a single campaign or line item can spend, independently of the balance. A campaign can have remaining budget while its balance is exhausted, or vice versa.

**Criteo Budget** — the default balance model. Balances under this model are created and managed in the Commerce Max UI, not via the API.

**Retailer Budget** — an alternative balance model where the retailer funds working media directly. Requires retailer authorization. Balances are read-only via the API — amounts and metadata are managed by Criteo on behalf of the retailer.

## Campaign concepts

**Buy Type** — determines how a line item acquires ad inventory. Three types: `Auction` (real-time CPC bidding, used by Sponsored Products), `PreferredDeals` (reserved audience targeting, CPM pricing, used by Onsite Display), and `Sponsorship` (100% share-of-voice takeover for a placement). Set at line item creation and cannot be changed.

**Attribution Window** — the period after an ad interaction (click or view) during which a subsequent purchase can be credited to that ad. Configured at the campaign level and applies to all line items in the campaign. Determines what appears in performance reports as attributed sales or revenue.

**Page Type** — the type of retailer page where an ad is served: Search, Category, Home, Product Detail Page (PDP), and others. Available as a dimension in performance reports and as a targeting filter on some line item types.

## Pricing models

**CPC (Cost per Click)** — the pricing model for Sponsored Products line items. You pay per click; the amount is determined by your bid in the auction.

**CPM (Cost per Mille)** — the pricing model for Onsite Display Preferred Deals line items. You pay per 1,000 impressions.
