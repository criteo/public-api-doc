---
title: Fill Rate Report
description: Supply-side fill rate and unfilled placement reporting for retailers.
---

Measures how many ad placements were successfully filled versus the total number of delivered placements. Use this to identify supply/demand gaps by placement, page type, category, or ad server.

Two companion reports cover different angles of the same inventory:

- **Fill Rate** — aggregated fill rate, coverage, and revenue metrics per placement
- **Unfilled Placements** — breakdown of *why* placements went unfilled (no demand, blocked, uncovered search terms, etc.)

## Prerequisites

- Authenticated as a supply account with scope `retail-media:read`
- Supply account ID

## Steps

Both endpoints are asynchronous — see [Async Patterns](/retail-media/docs/async-patterns).

### Fill Rate Report

#### 1. Submit

[→ `POST /retail-media/reports/fillrate`](/retail-media/reference/generate-async-fill-rate-report)

Provide `supplyAccountIds`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `dimensions`, and `metrics`.

Use `adServerType` to filter by ad server: `all`, `gam`, or `criteo`. Defaults to `all` if omitted.

**Returns:** `reportId` — used in steps 2 and 3.

#### 2. Poll for status

[→ `GET /retail-media/reports/{reportId}/status`](/retail-media/reference/get-async-export-status)

Poll until `status` is `success` or `failure`.

#### 3. Download

[→ `GET /retail-media/reports/{reportId}/output`](/retail-media/reference/get-async-export-output)

### Unfilled Placements Report

#### 1. Submit

[→ `POST /retail-media/reports/unfilled-placements`](/retail-media/reference/generate-async-unfilled-placements-report)

Same shape as the fill rate report. Returns a breakdown of unfilled reasons per placement.

<Info>
  See [Metrics & Dimensions (SSP)](/retail-media/docs/metrics-and-dimensions-ssp#fill-rate-unfilled-reasons) for the full list of supported unfilled reason metrics.
</Info>

**Returns:** `reportId` — used in steps 2 and 3.

#### 2. Poll for status

[→ `GET /retail-media/reports/{reportId}/status`](/retail-media/reference/get-async-export-status)

#### 3. Download

[→ `GET /retail-media/reports/{reportId}/output`](/retail-media/reference/get-async-export-output)
