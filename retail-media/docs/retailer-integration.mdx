This section covers everything a retailer needs to integrate with the Retail Media API. Follow the pages in order for a first-time setup.

---

## Standard integration (all retailers)

All retailers go through these three steps regardless of setup type:

1. **[Choose Your Consent Flow](/retail-media/docs/consent-flow-overview)** — Determine whether you need the standard consent flow or the Private Market programmatic flow before you start.
2. **[Discover Retailers and Pages](/retail-media/docs/retailer-search)** — Look up which retailers your account has access to and what page types are available for targeting.
3. **[Manage Store Inventory](/retail-media/docs/store-inventory)** — Push real-time product availability and pricing data at the store level to keep ad experiences accurate.

---

## Private Market extension

If you operate a Retail Media Private Market — managing brands or sellers under your supply account — complete these additional steps:

4. **[Grant Consent Programmatically](/retail-media/docs/private-market-programmatic-consent-granting)** — Set up the two-app model (Admin + Business app) to grant consent for demand accounts without requiring each brand to go through the portal.
5. **[Create and Manage Demand Accounts](/retail-media/docs/account-creation-and-management-private-market)** — Create brand and seller accounts within your Private Market and manage their access.

<Note>
  Private Market access requires Criteo to enable the correct permissions on your account. Contact your Criteo representative before starting steps 4 and 5.
</Note>
