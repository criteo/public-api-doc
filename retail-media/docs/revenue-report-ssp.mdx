Revenue visibility for supply account owners (retailers) across all advertisers, media platforms, and sales channels — including direct, indirect, and private market revenue.

## Prerequisites

- Authenticated as a supply account with scope `retail-media:read`
- Supply account ID

## Steps

This endpoint is asynchronous — see [Async Patterns](/retail-media/docs/async-patterns).

### 1. Submit

[→ `POST /retail-media/reports/revenue`](/retail-media/reference/generate-async-revenue-report)

Provide a supply account `id`, `startDate`, `endDate`, plus `metrics` and `dimensions`.

**Returns:** `reportId` — used in steps 2 and 3.

<Note>
  When `metrics` and `dimensions` arrays are provided, the `reportType` field is ignored.
</Note>

<Note>
  When using `clickMatchLevel` and `viewMatchLevel`, include the `attributionSettings`, `activityType`, and `skuRelation` dimensions for a clearer breakdown of click and view events by attribution window and SKU relationship.
</Note>

### 2. Poll for status

[→ `GET /retail-media/reports/{reportId}/status`](/retail-media/reference/get-async-export-status)

Poll until `status` is `success` or `failure`.

### 3. Download

[→ `GET /retail-media/reports/{reportId}/output`](/retail-media/reference/get-async-export-output)

## UI default attribution settings

To match the default view in the CMax UI, use these attribution parameters:

**Onsite Sponsored Products:**
- `clickAttributionWindow`: `30D`
- `clickMatchLevel`: `sameCategory`
- `viewAttributionWindow`: `1D`
- `viewMatchLevel`: `sameSku`

**Onsite Display:**
- `clickAttributionWindow`: `14D`
- `clickMatchLevel`: `sameCategory`
- `viewAttributionWindow`: `14D`
- `viewMatchLevel`: `sameCategory`

## Constraints

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Max date range | 100 days |
