The Partner Billing Report enables retailers to bill their retailer-contracted advertisers, manage invoicing, and track payments — particularly in private market setups. It also provides a way to verify retailer-related fees and media costs associated with campaigns managed through Commerce Max.

<Note>
  Generate and download partner billing reports. [→ API Reference](/retail-media/reference/create-partner-billing-report-request-v1)
</Note>

<Note>
  Learn more in the Account & Billing section of the [CMax Help Center](https://help.retailmedia.criteo.com/kb/en/account-billing-127261).
</Note>

## How it works

The report is generated asynchronously — submit a request, poll for status, and download the output when ready. See [Async Patterns](/retail-media/docs/async-patterns).

Reports are generated at line item granularity: one row per Demand account × Campaign × Line Item.

## Constraints

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Max date range | 31 days |
| Data processing | Daily batch — same-day data is not available |
| File availability | At least 1 hour after generation |
