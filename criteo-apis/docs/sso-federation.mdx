## Overview

SSO federation lets your users access Criteo platforms using your organization's existing identity system — no separate Criteo credentials required. Once configured, users on your federated domain are redirected to your identity provider (IdP) login page when they access Criteo.

Criteo uses **SAML 2.0** for federation. Two systems exchange metadata to establish trust:

| Term | Role | Example systems |
|------|------|-----------------|
| **Service Provider (SP)** | Criteo — the application your users want to access | — |
| **Identity Provider (IdP)** | Your organization's identity system, which authenticates users and sends Criteo a SAML assertion | Okta, Azure AD, ADFS |

SSO federation is available for the following Criteo platforms:

- **Commerce Growth** — `marketing.criteo.com`
- **Retail Media (CMax and CYield)** — `retailmedia.criteo.com`
- **Commerce Grid** — `commercegrid.criteo.com`

SSO federation is available to all clients regardless of tier. Onboarding is subject to a waitlist based on R&D team capacity and client availability — Criteo currently integrates one client per month. Contact your Criteo representative to join the waitlist.

***

## Before you start

Gather the following before initiating an SSO federation request:

| What | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Domains to federate** | The email domain(s) your users will log in with (e.g. `yourcompany.com`). You can start with one and add more later. |
| **Technical contact(s)** | The member(s) of your IT or identity team who will handle the IdP configuration. |
| **Test users** | A short list of email addresses to validate the integration before going live. |
| **Test account** | If you have multiple Criteo advertiser accounts, identify which one will be used for testing. Criteo needs the account name to invite the test user if they are not already associated with it. |
| **Target go-live date** | The date you want SSO active for all users. |
| **IdP type** | Which identity provider your organization uses, and which SAML attribute you will send for user matching. Criteo recommends `emailaddress`. |
| **IdP metadata file** | Your IdP's metadata XML file — it contains your signing certificate, the IdP Entity ID, and the SSO URL Location that Criteo needs to establish trust. You will share this with Criteo during onboarding. |

***

## What Criteo provides

Once onboarding begins, Criteo generates and shares a **Service Provider (SP) metadata file**. Use it to configure the Criteo application in your IdP. It contains the following three values:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Audience URI (Entity ID)** | Unique identifier for Criteo's SSO service |
| **Reply URL (ACS URL)** | The destination URL where your IdP sends the SAML assertion after a successful login |
| **X509 Certificate** | Criteo's signing certificate |

## Onboarding process

### Step 1 — Request onboarding

Contact your Criteo representative to initiate the SSO federation process. They will add you to the waitlist and confirm your expected onboarding date based on current capacity.

Prior to the technical meeting (Step 2), your Criteo account manager will share:

- The **SP metadata file** you will need to configure your IdP, which includes the following values:
  - **Audience URI (Entity ID)** — unique identifier for Criteo's SSO service
  - **Assertion Consumer Service URL (Reply URL)** — where your IdP sends the SAML assertion after login
  - **NameID format** — the format Criteo expects for the user identifier in the assertion
- A list of information Criteo needs from you, so you can come prepared

### Step 2 — Technical discussion

Your IT or identity team joins a technical call with Criteo to align on:

- Which domains to federate now and which may be added later
- Which IdP you use and which SAML attribute will be sent for matching
- The initial list of test user emails to enable first
- Which Criteo advertiser account will be used for testing (if you have multiple accounts, Criteo needs the account name to invite the test user)
- Your target go-live date

### Step 3 — You share IdP metadata

Once your IdP configuration is ready, share the following with Criteo:

- Your **IdP metadata XML file** — it contains your signing certificate, the IdP Entity ID, and the SSO URL Location Criteo needs. It may be delivered in an encrypted ZIP.
- The final list of **test users** to enable first

### Step 4 — Test activation

Criteo enables SSO for your agreed test users only — not for all users on the domain yet. You will receive by email a URL to begin testing. The URL is the address of the Criteo platform where your account is located:

- `marketing.criteo.com` for Commerce Growth
- `retailmedia.criteo.com` for Retail Media (CMax / CYield)
- `commercegrid.criteo.com` for Commerce Grid

<Note>
  SSO is activated per user during this phase. Your other users continue to log in as normal until go-live.
</Note>

### Step 5 — Testing

Your test users log in via the URL shared in Step 4 and verify:

- They are redirected to your IdP login page
- After login, they are redirected to their Criteo account
- They can see the right accounts and data

If anything is wrong, share screenshots or error messages with Criteo. The integration can be reverted on request during this phase.

<Note>
  **Preprod before prod:** If your organization runs a preprod environment, you may first test SSO with preprod IdP metadata. Once preprod is stable, you will go through Steps 3–5 again with your production metadata before the final go-live.
</Note>

### Step 6 — Go live

Once all test users can log in successfully, Criteo activates SSO for all users on your federated domain(s) on the agreed date.

<Warning>
  Go-live is **all-or-nothing per domain.** Once SSO is activated for a domain, all users on that domain must log in via SSO to access Criteo.
</Warning>

If something goes wrong after go-live, contact Criteo to roll back or fix the configuration.

## Ongoing maintenance

Some fields are defined once and rarely need to change. Others require periodic updates during the lifetime of the integration.

### Rarely changed

These are set during initial onboarding and stay stable:

- SP Entity ID
- SP ACS / Reply URL
- IdP Entity ID
- SAML attribute used for user matching

### Updated over time

These are expected to change and should be kept current:

| Field | Why it changes |
|-------|---------------|
| **Criteo's SP signing certificate** | Valid for 5 years from issuance. Criteo will notify you when a renewal is needed. |
| **Your IdP signing certificate** | Lifetime varies by your IdP configuration. You must rotate it before it expires — share the updated metadata file with Criteo in advance. |
| **Federated email domains** | You may add new domains or remove old ones after go-live |
| **IdP metadata** | May need updating if you migrate IdP systems or change environments |

To update any of these fields after go-live, contact your Criteo representative. Your account manager will coordinate with the R&D team to apply the change.
