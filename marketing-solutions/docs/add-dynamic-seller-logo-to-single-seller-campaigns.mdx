Dynamic Seller Logo lets Single-Seller campaigns in Marketplace Performance Outcomes (MPO) display the seller's logo instead of the marketplace logo, so the creative reflects the seller being promoted.

This page covers how seller logos work, the product feed requirements, and how to prepare your logo assets.

### Before you start

- Single-Seller campaigns are already set up for your account.
- A seller logo URL is supplied through the product feed as extra data.

Criteo applies the logo setting to the Single-Seller template. Single-Seller ad sets generated from that template inherit the setting, so there's nothing to configure per seller.

### How seller logos work

| Behavior | Marketplace logo | Seller logo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand shown | Marketplace brand | Seller brand |
| Source | Marketplace configuration | `extra_seller_logo_url` in the product feed as extra data |
| Supported campaigns | All MPO campaigns | Single-Seller campaigns only |
| Ad format | Dynamic Ads | Dynamic Ads |
| Logo fallback | Not applicable | If the seller logo is missing, empty, invalid, or cannot be rendered, the marketplace logo is used instead |

Seller logos are supported in Dynamic Ads for MPO campaigns and only on Single-Seller campaigns. Multi-Seller campaigns are not supported.

Clicking the seller logo opens the top-ranked product's landing page. That's the product our recommendation engine scores highest for each shopper. You don't need to supply a separate seller or storefront URL.

### Send the seller logo in your product feed

Add the seller logo URL to each product using the `extra_seller_logo_url` field. Use the same logo URL for all products belonging to the same seller.

### Logo specifications

Seller logos follow the same requirements as any logo in a Criteo creative.

### Add the logo to a live Single-Seller campaign

Once Criteo has configured your template:

1. Add the seller logo URL to each product using the `extra_seller_logo_url` field.
2. Check that the value is consistent across all products for that seller.
3. Create and manage seller campaigns through your existing Single-Seller MPO workflow. No extra call is needed for the logo.
4. Review the delivered creative and confirm that the seller logo renders as expected.

If the marketplace logo appears instead, the feed value is most likely missing, empty, or unreachable for that product.

For campaign creation and budget management, see [MPO Single-Seller Campaigns](https://developers.criteo.com/marketing-solutions/docs/single-seller-campaigns).

### What to do now

If you want to use seller logos in your Single-Seller campaigns, speak to your Criteo contact about enabling it on your template. In the meantime, check whether your sellers have logo assets that meet the logo specifications above—that's usually the longest lead time on your side.
